Handoff

Privacy Policy

Effective September 11, 2026

The short version: Handoff stores your family's schedule on your own device. We don't collect it, we can't see it, and we don't share it with anyone.

Who we are

Handoff is a family coordination app that helps households plan weekly schedules, pickups, drop-offs, appointments, chores and handoffs. This policy explains what information the app handles and how.

Information the app stores

To work, Handoff keeps the information you enter:

  • your family name and the names, roles and colors of family members (including children)
  • calendar events, handoffs, tasks, availability, notes and settings

All of this is saved only on the device you are using — in your browser's local storage (also when the app is installed to your home screen). It is not transmitted to us or to any server we operate.

Information we collect

None. Handoff has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. We do not receive your name, email address, location, contacts, or any of the content you enter.

Network requests

The app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts when a connection is available. That request reveals your IP address to Google, as any web request does; it carries none of your family data. Google's handling of that request is described in the Google Privacy Policy. No other network requests are made.

Children

Handoff is designed for parents and caregivers. Children's names that you enter are stored on your device alongside the rest of your family's plan and are never sent to us. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Sharing

When you use Share brief, the app hands the text of your weekly brief to your device's share sheet (Messages, Mail, etc.). What happens next is governed by the app you choose to share with. Handoff itself does not send the brief anywhere.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Erase all data in the app, or clear the site's data in your browser. Because nothing is stored outside your device, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Future features

If we add optional features that require sending data off your device — such as syncing between family members, calendar connections, or notifications — they will be opt-in, clearly explained inside the app, and this policy will be updated before they launch.

Not medical or emergency advice

Handoff is a coordination tool. It is not a replacement for professional medical advice or emergency services.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page and update the effective date above.

Contact

Handoff is currently a private family tool. If you have questions about this policy, contact the person who shared the app with you.
